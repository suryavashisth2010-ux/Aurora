Ingredient Provenance

We source
one thing
at a time.

Every Aurora formula begins with a single question: what does this skin actually need? Not a trend. Not a marketing brief. We spend months sourcing each ingredient from growers who share our fixation on quality over yield.

Our rosehip comes from a 40-hectare cooperative in Chilean Patagonia. The marula is cold-pressed within 24 hours of harvest in Namibia. The bakuchiol is extracted from babchi seeds using no synthetic solvents.
